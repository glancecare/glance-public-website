## Introduction

Revenue leakage in healthcare is becoming an increasingly significant issue in Saudi Arabia as the healthcare industry faces growing demands for efficiency and regulatory compliance. With the shift towards value-based care and the implementation of Vision 2030, healthcare providers are under pressure to optimize their revenue cycles while maintaining high standards of patient care. Without reliable tools in place, healthcare organizations risk inefficiencies that lead to claim rejections, underpayments, and operational delays, ultimately impacting their bottom line.

In this case study, we explore how Glance Care’s suite of solutions successfully reduced revenue leakage by 29% for a leading healthcare provider in Riyadh. By identifying critical pain points in the revenue cycle and implementing tailored solutions, we helped streamline operations, improve claim acceptance rates, and ensure regulatory compliance, all while enhancing the overall patient care experience.

## Impact

* **Revenue Leakage Reduction:** A significant 29% reduction in revenue leakage was achieved by addressing initial claim rejection rates and inefficiencies in the claims process.
    
* **Faster Approvals:** Glance’s PreAuth solution helped reduce claim rejections and sped up the approval process, decreasing patient wait times and increasing satisfaction.
    
* **Improved Operational Efficiency:** Automation and streamlined workflows resulted in reduced administrative costs and higher resource allocation efficiency.
