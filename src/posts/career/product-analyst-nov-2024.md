The ideal candidate will possess a strong background in health tech, and revenue cycle management, specifically focusing on AI decision support systems, data analytics, and business intelligence. You will play a key role in driving product R&D improvements and strategic decision-making by providing data-driven insights and analysis.

Glance care is at the forefront of healthcare innovation, leveraging advanced technologies to enhance clinical decision-making and operational efficiency in the healthcare industry. Our AI-powered decision support systems, health and financial data analytics, business intelligence, and record auditing solutions empower healthcare organizations to deliver superior patient care.

- Product Analysis
- Market & Customer Research
- Performance Monitoring
- Feedback Analysis
- Collaboration
- Reporting & Presentation

#### Qualifications:

- Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field such as Health Informatics, Data Science, computer sciences, Business Analytics, Medicine, or a related discipline.
- Proven experience in a product analysis role, preferably within the health tech sector, with a focus on data analytics and decision support systems.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to interpret complex data sets into actionable business insights.
- Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to convey complex information to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Familiarity with healthcare regulations and standards is a plus.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
- Advance understanding of the Saudi healthcare market.
