We are looking for a highly skilled and motivated Product Manager to lead the development and management of our SaaS healthcare products. The ideal candidate will have a deep understanding of the healthcare sector, experience with SaaS solutions, and extensive knowledge of the Saudi Arabian market. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to define product strategy, gather requirements, and ensure our products meet the needs of healthcare providers, patients, and stakeholders.

#### About Us:
We are a fast-growing health tech startup focused on delivering advanced healthcare solutions tailored to the Saudi Arabian market. Our mission is to leverage cutting-edge technology to improve health outcomes, streamline healthcare services, and enhance patient experiences. If you are passionate about transforming healthcare and want to make a meaningful impact in Saudi Arabia, we would love to have you on our team.

#### Responsibilities:

- Develop and execute a product strategy aligned with business objectives and tailored to the Saudi healthcare market.
- Conduct market research to understand healthcare trends, customer needs, and regulatory requirements in Saudi Arabia.
- Collaborate with engineering, design, and data science teams to define, prioritize, and launch new product features and enhancements.
- Engage with customers, healthcare professionals, and industry experts to gather feedback and translate insights into product requirements.
- Monitor and analyze product performance, using data to drive continuous improvement.
- Own the product roadmap, ensuring alignment with strategic goals and market demand.
- Stay updated on competitive landscape, emerging technologies, and local market trends in health tech.
- Work closely with the sales and marketing teams to develop product positioning, messaging, and go-to-market strategies.

#### Requirements:

- Proven experience as a Product Manager in the SaaS and/or healthcare technology sector.
- In-depth knowledge of the Saudi Arabian healthcare market, including regulatory landscape and customer needs.
- Strong understanding of healthcare software solutions, especially within a SaaS environment.
- Exceptional analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate across departments and with external stakeholders.
- Experience with agile methodologies and product management tools (e.g., JIRA, Aha!).

#### Preferred Qualifications:

- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Healthcare Management, Computer Science, or a related field; advanced degree a plus.
- Arabic language skills are a plus.
- Prior experience in a health tech startup or similar high-growth environment.

#### Why Join Us?

- Be part of a mission-driven team that is transforming healthcare in Saudi Arabia.
- Work in a collaborative and innovative environment with opportunities for growth.
- Competitive salary and benefits package.

If you’re passionate about healthcare innovation and excited about shaping the future of health tech in Saudi Arabia, apply now!
