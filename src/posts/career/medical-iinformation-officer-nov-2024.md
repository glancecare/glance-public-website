#### About Us:
Glance care is a leading health tech company dedicated to enhancing healthcare delivery through innovative technology solutions.
Our mission is to support healthcare providers with cutting-edge tools that improve clinical outcomes and operational efficiency.
We are seeking a qualified Medical Quality Officer to oversee our medical operations and ensure the highest standards of quality and safety.

#### Position Overview:
The Medical Information Officer plays a critical leadership role in overseeing the quality and governance of medical data, spearheading research and development initiatives, and guiding our product development and technological architecture. The ideal candidate will have a robust clinical background combined with deep expertise in health informatics, product development, and medical ontologies.

#### Key Responsibilities:
- Lead strategic initiatives to improve medical data quality and ensure data integrity across platforms.
- Oversee and direct research and development projects, aligning them with organizational goals and innovation pathways.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and implement next-generation health tech products and solutions.
- Serve as a thought leader and advocate for technology adoption and data-driven decision-making within the organization.
- Develop and manage medical ontologies that enhance data interoperability and usability.
- Establish and foster partnerships with external stakeholders, including healthcare providers, research institutions, and industry partners.
- Ensure compliance with regulatory standards and best practices in medical data management and informatics.


#### Qualifications:
- Medical Degree (MD or equivalent) with board certification.
- Advanced degree or certification in Health Informatics or a related field.
- Proven experience in product development, R&D, and technological architecture in a health tech setting.
- Strong knowledge and application of medical ontologies and data governance frameworks.
- Exceptional leadership, communication, and project management skills.
- Experience in a high-growth, fast-paced environment is highly desirable.

- Flexible, remote working arrangements
- Opportunity to work with a team of passionate professionals committed to improving healthcare
- The chance to make a significant impact on healthcare quality through innovative technology
