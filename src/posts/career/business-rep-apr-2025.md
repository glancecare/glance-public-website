#### About Us:

Glance Care is a health-tech/insurance tech company based in Riyadh.
We specialize in innovating medical support systems.
Our mission is to bring business growth to the Saudi healthcare industry.

#### Role Description:

Glance Care is seeking a motivated Junior Product Representative to join our growing team! In this role, you will introduce our cutting-edge health tech product to physicians, demonstrate its value, and support client success in a clinical setting. If you have a passion for healthcare and technology, we’d love to meet you!

#### Key Responsibilities:

- Visit clinics and hospitals to meet with physicians and healthcare professionals
- Present and explain our health tech product effectively to drive interest and conversions
- Support doctors and clinical staff during product onboarding and usage
- Gather feedback and communicate client needs to the internal team
- Assist in maintaining strong, ongoing relationships with clients

#### Qualifications:

- Bachelor’s degree OR diploma in a medical-related field (e.g., Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Health Informatics)
- Previous experience as an ICD10 coder, in Revenue Cycle Management (RCM), or as a pharmaceutical sales rep is a plus
- Strong communication and presentation skills
- Enthusiasm for healthcare technology
- Ability to build rapport with physicians and healthcare staff
- Willingness to travel locally for physician visits

- Be part of a dynamic team delivering impactful health solutions
- Competitive salary and benefits
- Opportunity to grow with an innovative company
